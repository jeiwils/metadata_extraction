You are cataloguing a book from images of its first pages (cover, title page,
the back of the title page). Extract metadata as it appears on these pages.
Leave a field empty ("" or []) rather than guessing.

title
- The main title as printed on the cover or title page, without the subtitle.
- Transcribe it; do not describe the document.

subtitle
- The rest of the title, whether it follows a colon or sits on its own line
  beneath the title. For example, a cover reading
    "Monitoring Report 118"
    "Water Quality in Upland Reservoirs"
  gives title "Monitoring Report 118" and subtitle
  "Water Quality in Upland Reservoirs". Several lines of subtitle are joined
  with ": ".
- Not a series statement, a date or report period, or an edition statement:
  those have their own fields below.

author
- Every person named as an author, in printed order, each written as
  "Last, First". Always convert, even when the page prints "First Last":
    "Stephanie P Jones"   -> "Jones, Stephanie P"
    "Thijs van Rens"      -> "van Rens, Thijs"
    "See-Yu Chan"         -> "Chan, See-Yu"
    "MV Padma Srivastava" -> "Srivastava, MV Padma"
  If you cannot tell which part is the surname, use the last word.
- Exclude translators and illustrators. Include editors only if no author is
  named.
- If no person is named, give the organisation responsible as the only
  author, using its name from the copyright or "published by" statement
  (for example "Fera Science Ltd"), not a brand or product name.

edition
- The edition statement as printed, such as "Second edition" or "Revised
  edition". Empty if there is none.

date
- The year of publication, as four digits. Take it from "Published",
  "First published", the copyright line, or the date on the cover or title
  page.
- It is NOT the period the work covers, or the dates of a survey, test or
  project. A report covering "November 2023 - January 2024" with
  "(c) 2024" was published in 2024.
- If several years appear, prefer the copyright or first-published year of
  this edition.

place
- The city of publication, from the imprint or the publisher's address
  (for example "London" or "Peterborough"). Empty if none is given.

publisher
- The organisation that published the document: from "Published by", the
  copyright line or the imprint.
- Name only: no address, postcode, telephone number, email, website, or
  abbreviation in brackets after the name.
- For an article or report in a journal, the publisher is the organisation
  that publishes the journal, not the journal's title and not the group that
  wrote the report. A report in "Health Technology Assessment" from the
  "NIHR Journals Library", written by "BMJ Technology Assessment Group", is
  published by "NIHR Journals Library".
- When the page names a centre or department within a larger organisation,
  give both: "Centre for Analysis of Social Exclusion, London School of
  Economics".
- Not a series name (such as "Discussion Paper", "Working Papers" or
  "Economics Research Papers"), not a funder, and not a brand when the
  organisation's own name is given. A paper in a university department's
  working-paper series is published by that department or university.
- Use the full name rather than an abbreviation when both appear.

series
- The name of a series the book belongs to, without its number: for
  "Book 8 of the Bite Back series", "Bite Back"; for "Natural England
  Commissioned Report NECR508", "Natural England Commissioned Report".
  Empty if there is none.

series_number
- The book's number in that series: "8", "NECR508". Empty if there is none.

notes
- Up to 3 short notes of other facts printed on these pages that a cataloguer
  would record, each a short phrase: for example the period a report covers
  ("Report covers December 2023-January 2024") or who commissioned it.
  Do not repeat the title, authors, publisher, date, edition or series.
  Do not summarise or describe the content. Empty if there is nothing to note.
